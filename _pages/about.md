# 👋 About Me
I am currently pursuing my Ph.D. at the School of Advanced Manufacturing and Robotics, Peking University, under the supervision of [Prof. Zhongkui Li (李忠奎)](https://www.zhongkuili-pku.com/cn/), and collaborating closely with [Prof. Meng Guo (国萌)](https://mengguo.github.io/personal_site/index.html).
Prior to that, I received my B.S. degree in Theoretical and Applied Mechanics from the College of Engineering at Peking University in 2023.

My research interests include <span style="font-weight: bold; color: #4e88c7;">multi-robot coordination</span>, <span style="font-weight: bold; color: #4e88c7;">motion and path planning</span>, <span style="font-weight: bold; color: #4e88c7;">trajectory optimization</span> and <span style="font-weight: bold; color: #4e88c7;">robotics</span>.
In particular, my current work focuses on decentralized trajectory planning and coordination for multi-robot systems in obstacle-rich environments.

🤖️ My long-term goal is to unlock the underestimated potential of multi-robot systems in real-world applications. 
I am particularly interested in enabling robots to move, collaborate, and interact with each other in more natural, efficient, and intelligent ways, ultimately bridging the gap between theoretical research and practical deployment.
